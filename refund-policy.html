Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The SKelite HR Toolkit is a digital product. Access is given by sharing a private Google Drive folder with the email address you provide at checkout.

Cancellation

You may cancel for a full refund at any time before folder access has been
shared with you. Write to hr@skelite-talent.com and we will refund you without
asking for a reason.

Refunds

Because these are editable digital files that cannot be returned once received, we are unable to refund an order after folder access has been shared, except in the cases below.

We will refund you in full, or correct the order, whichever you prefer, if:

  • your access has not arrived within one working day of payment
  • you were given the wrong pack
  • a file is corrupt or will not open

Timeline

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method and normally reach you within 5 to 7 working days. The exact timing after we initiate the refund depends on your bank or card issuer.

Services

This policy covers the HR Toolkit only. Recruitment and consultancy engagements are governed by the separate written agreement for that engagement, including any replacement guarantee, which is agreed in writing before work begins.
