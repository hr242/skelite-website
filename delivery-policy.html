Delivery Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The SKelite HR Toolkit is delivered digitally. No physical goods are shipped, so no shipping charges apply and no delivery address is needed.

How delivery works

After your payment succeeds, we share a private Google Drive folder with the email address you entered at checkout, usually within 4 working hours. Orders placed in the evening or on a Sunday arrive the next working morning.

The folder is yours. You can download everything from it and keep it.

What you receive

Editable Microsoft Word and Excel files. Not locked PDFs. The number of files depends on the pack or bundle you bought, and is stated on the product page before you pay.

Before you pay

Please check that the email address you enter is correct and that you can access it. We send access to that address and nowhere else.

If it has not arrived

If your access has not reached you within one working day of payment, contact us and we will sort it immediately, or refund you in full if you would rather.
